## Phase 4 acceptance basis

Phase 4 is accepted because:

1. Workstream 1 established exact-revision interaction contracts and a deterministic local reference shell.
2. Workstream 2 produced pinned Chromium keyboard, focus, workflow, failure, responsive, and zero-external-request evidence.
3. Workstream 3 established deterministic read-only workspace contracts, a replaceable static reader, exact local exports, unresolved advisory candidates, and explicit rollback boundaries.
4. Workstream 4 reused those contracts and the unchanged reader for one materially different Catalase assay-methodology fixture.
5. The original recommender workspace remains byte-identical and is the rollback baseline.
6. Both fixture paths preserve separate Principia status, exact Atlas revisions, visible warnings, non-graph operation, and non-authoritative browser state.
7. Closure evidence passed fourteen deterministic gates and twenty named negative authority cases.
8. No report, workspace, browser, selector, or runtime artifact obtained canonical, lifecycle, review, candidate-resolution, merge, release, repository, production, deployment, or cross-repository authority.

## Accepted conclusion

The evidence is sufficient to conclude that Atlas has a coherent, deterministic, local-first foundation for:

- exact-revision knowledge identity;
- provenance and lifecycle visibility;
- bounded advisory retrieval;
- read-only research-workspace composition;
- replaceable static presentation;
- explicit failure semantics;
- offline, status-separate Principia references;
- deterministic export, validation, and rollback.

The evidence is not sufficient to claim universal contract generality, production retrieval quality, a final product interface, deployment readiness, human usability validation, accessibility certification, or live cross-repository synchronization.

The first Principia action must be inspection, not speculative expansion. It must identify:

- Principia’s current authoritative phase and accepted history;
- unfinished or stale workstreams;
- its content, model, investigation, dossier, and design-challenge contracts;
- existing Atlas identifiers and bridge seams;
- duplicated or contradictory material;
- the smallest complete vertical path that can consume exact Atlas revisions without mutating Atlas.

After inspection, Principia should implement one bounded end-to-end pathway from an Atlas question, claim, evidence, source, or model into a Principia explanation, investigation, model, system analysis, or design challenge. Any Atlas change must be separated into its own bounded proposal.
